Over the course of the semester, my perspective on using AI in programming has become much more positive and practical. I initially viewed AI as mainly for convenience, but I came to see it as a powerful learning and troubleshooting tool. Instead of waiting for office hours or struggling for long periods on small issues, I was able to quickly identify errors, understand why they occurred, and continue making progress. This allowed me to learn concepts faster and reinforce them through hands-on practice rather than getting stuck.

In the future, I plan to continue using AI as a support tool in programming and coursework. I will use it to help debug code, review design patterns, come up with more efficient solutions and validate my understanding of concepts before submitting work. While I still plan to do my own thinking and problem-solving first, AI will be a valuable resource to accelerate learning, improve code quality, and help clarify difficult topics more efficiently.
